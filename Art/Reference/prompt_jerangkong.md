# Prompt konsep Jerangkong (boss Ep 1)

Brief: [docs/05](../../docs/05-daftar-episode-boss.md#arc-1--kebangkitan-ep-110) (kerangka yang terus menyusun ulang dirinya; hancurkan tengkoraknya), [docs/16](../../docs/16-panduan-arc-1.md#26-aturan-skala) (±0,7× tinggi Jayadwara ≈ 31 m), [docs/17](../../docs/17-skenario-arc-1.md#adegan-6--kebangkitan--seq) (bangkit dari galian, energi Kala merah di sela rusuk).
Gaya: stylized semi-realistis, sama dengan Jayadwara & Aksa. Seram tapi **tanpa gore** (tanpa daging, darah, organ).

Creature: Jerangkong, a giant walking skeleton from Javanese folklore, awakened from an ancient temple excavation by dark red cursed energy. Bones are old and earthy: ivory to brown, cracked, caked with soil and dried mud, a few thin roots and bits of moss stuck in the joints, small fragments of ancient carved stone embedded in the bones. Tattered remains of an ancient faded batik cloth tied around the waist. A few corroded old bronze ornaments on the wrists and upper arms, like an ancient Javanese nobleman.

Cursed energy: thin dark red glowing wisps hold the joints together, visible between the vertebrae, inside the ribcage, and in the shoulder and hip joints, as if the bones could fall apart and pull themselves back together. Both eye sockets glow dark red. The skull is clearly the weak point: a glowing red crack across the forehead.

Important: no flesh, no blood, no gore, no organs, no muscles. Not cute, but not grotesque either: eerie and readable, suitable for a teen-rated game. No blue light, no silver.

Style: clean stylized semi-realistic game creature concept art, bold readable silhouette, chunky simplified shapes rather than anatomically exact bones, consistent design across all views, suitable as a 3D modeling reference.
