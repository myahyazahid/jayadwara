# Prompt konsep Tuyul (boss Ep 2 + kawanan kecil)

Brief: [docs/05](../../docs/05-daftar-episode-boss.md#arc-1--kebangkitan-ep-110) (kawanan tuyul lalu bergabung jadi satu raksasa), [docs/16](../../docs/16-panduan-arc-1.md#ep-2--tangan-tangan-kecil--tuyul) (jahil, lincah, mencuri properti warga), [docs/27](../../docs/27-rencana-model-tuyul.md) (arahan desain bagian 2).
Gaya: **realistis** (docs/README, 14 Okt 2026), sama dengan Jayadwara2, Aksa2, dan Jerangkong. Jahil dan lincah, **bukan horor**, **tanpa gore**, dan **jelas bukan anak manusia**.

## 1. Turnaround boss + kawanan (rasio 2:1)

```
Monster character concept sheet for a realistic 3D action game. Left side: three orthographic views of the same creature side by side, FRONT view, SIDE view (left profile), BACK view, same scale, feet aligned on one ground line, neutral A-pose with arms angled away from the body, mouth slightly open, eyes open. Right side: a large close-up of the head from a 3/4 angle, and a small scale chart showing the creature next to a red-and-gold armored giant hero silhouette (the creature is about the same height as the hero). Plain flat light-gray background, flat even lighting, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no logos.

Creature: Tuyul, a mischievous money-stealing spirit from Javanese folklore, grown huge by dark red cursed energy. Short stocky body with a very large head (about 3 heads tall), round pot belly, long nimble arms with quick agile hands and separated fingers, small fast feet. Skin is pale grey-green like old wax, NOT a human skin tone. Big pointed ears, huge dark eyes without human pupils, a wide cheeky grin with small pointed teeth and a visible gap between the lips, a smooth bald head with a few fine cracks. Naughty, playful expression, clearly a monster and clearly not a human child. A tattered dirty loincloth, a small worn sack hanging at the side, and a few banknotes and old coins stuck to the body and loincloth.

Cursed energy: thin dark red glowing veins on the chest and arms, glowing dark red eyes, and a small soft red glow on top of the head.

Important: no flesh wounds, no blood, no gore, no organs. Cheeky and a little eerie, not horrifying; suitable for a teen-rated game. No blue light, no silver.

Style: realistic game creature concept art with believable skin texture and anatomy, bold readable silhouette, consistent design across all views, suitable as a 3D modeling reference.
```

## 2. Versi kawanan kecil (opsional, rasio 1:1)

```
Concept art of a small mischievous spirit creature for a realistic 3D action game, same design as a giant version: about 1 meter tall, pale grey-green waxy skin (not human), very large bald head, big pointed ears, huge dark eyes, cheeky grin with small pointed teeth and a gap between the lips, round pot belly, tattered loincloth, a small sack, a few banknotes stuck to the body, thin dark red glowing veins and glowing dark red eyes. Front view and 3/4 view side by side, running pose and standing pose, plain light-gray background, flat lighting, no cast shadows, no text. Playful, not horrifying. No gore, no blood, no blue light, clearly not a human child.
```

## 3. Tips Tripo (pelajaran Aksa2, Jerangkong, Jayadwara2)

- **A-pose**, tangan terpisah dari badan, **jari terpisah** (jari menempel di Jerangkong), kaki sedikit terbuka dan menapak.
- **Mata terbuka, bibir punya celah** (mata dan mulut hanya dilukis di Aksa2 menghentikan rig wajah).
- Cawat dan kantong sebagai bagian terpisah dari paha/pinggul bila bisa (kalau menyatu, Hermes mencatatnya di K0).
- Hindari bayangan terpanggang di tekstur (minta flat-lit bila Tripo menyediakan); tanpa merah basah di kulit.
- Simpan GLB asli; salin ke `Hermes/Art/tuyul/Source/tuyul_tripo_v01.glb` (tugas Hermes).

## Cek setelah gambar jadi
- Siluet kepala besar + perut buncit terbaca dari jauh.
- Tidak terbaca sebagai anak manusia (warna kulit, telinga, mata).
- Merah gelap hanya sebagai pendar; tanpa gore; tanpa biru/silver.
- Tinggi boss = tinggi Jayadwara di chart (T3 di docs/27).
- Simpan hasil di `docs/concept-art/` (mis. `tuyul_v01.webp`).
